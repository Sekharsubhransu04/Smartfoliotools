Why Use the EMI Comparator?

Choosing the right home loan can save you lakhs of rupees over its tenure. The Smartfoliotools EMI Comparator lets you compare two loan offers side by side so you can find the best deal with confidence. Simply input the lender name, principal amount, interest rate, tenure, and processing fee for each offer — the tool instantly calculates and compares the monthly EMI, total interest, and total cost (including processing fee) for both options.

The comparator automatically highlights the winner with a 'Better Deal' badge and a green highlight, so you know at a glance which offer saves you more money. A visual bar chart displays both offers together for easy comparison. It's perfect for home buyers choosing between banks like SBI, HDFC, ICICI, Axis, Bank of Baroda, LIC HFL, Kotak Mahindra, and others.

Here's the real eye-opener: a mere 0.5% interest rate difference on a ₹50 lakh, 20-year loan can mean ₹3–5 lakh more in total interest. Many borrowers overlook this because they focus only on the monthly EMI. And the processing fee — typically ₹10,000 to ₹50,000 — is often ignored entirely. This tool includes it for a true apples-to-apples comparison. No login needed, no data stored — just instant, side-by-side results right in your browser.
